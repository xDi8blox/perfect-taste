# Perfect Taste Catering & Decoration: sample website

A design proposal for **Perfect Taste Catering & Decoration**, No 16 Alayaki Street, Orile-Iganmu, Lagos.

Live preview: https://xdiablo.xyz/perfect-taste/

Sample by Diablo, [xdiablo.xyz](https://xdiablo.xyz).

- Plain HTML/CSS/JS, mobile-first, with no build step.
- Photos: public Facebook posts mirrored on [Bakerias](https://www.bakerias.com/NG/Orile-Iganmu/109713544619868/Perfect-taste-catering-and-decoration), downloaded into `assets/img/`. One watermarked reshare (“Cook with Temmy”) was not used.
- Brand emblem and service list (cakes, small chops, event decorations) come from the public Perfect Taste Kitchen profile card (same WhatsApp number and address).
- Copy draws on their public posts and page text. Package prices are not published, so the site uses “Ask for price”.
- Opening hours, decoration photos, cake photos, testimonials and any stats were not available publicly and are not invented.
- The “How to order” steps and enquiry form are a suggested layout for the website; they open WhatsApp and do not store messages here.
